Spansk nyvalg i november

Spanias statsminister Pedro Sanchez utskriver nyvalg til 29. november etter at regjeringens boligreformer led nederlag.

Les mer

Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez on Monday called an early election for November 29. Facing the electorate now could be risky, as Sanchez's Socialist Party is trailing in opinion polls. Sánchez says Spain will head to the polls on 29 November after recent defeats on measures aimed at addressing the country's housing crisis. The Spanish prime minister had repeatedly insisted he would serve out his four-year term, with elections scheduled for 2027. The move comes after a major defeat for his coalition's proposed housing reforms.

USAs bombefly trekkes ut fra britisk base

Pentagon har trukket alle amerikanske B-1-bombefly tilbake fra RAF Fairford i Storbritannia etter en sikkerhetshendelse.

Les mer

The Pentagon confirmed on Sunday that all US B-1 bombers deployed to Britain’s RAF Fairford had returned home following a security incident that prompted nearby evacuations. Police found no explosives after reports of suspicious activity at the base last Sunday. Five London-based British men arrested were subsequently released on bail. No reason has been given for the withdrawal by the BBC, while DW notes it follows a suspected terrorist plot targeting the UK's Fairford airbase.
